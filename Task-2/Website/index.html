MODULE 02

What is Phishing?

Understand how phishing attacks work and what attackers want.

🎣
DEFINITION

Phishing in simple words

Phishing is a cyberattack where an attacker pretends to be a trusted person, company or service to trick a victim into revealing sensitive information or performing an unsafe action.

Example: You receive an email saying your account will be blocked. It asks you to click a link and verify your password.
ATTACKER OBJECTIVES

What are criminals looking for?

🔑
Credentials

Passwords, usernames and login sessions.

💳
Financial Information

Card, banking and payment information.

👤
Personal Information

Identity and other private information.

🏢
Business Access

Corporate accounts, systems and data.

ATTACK LIFECYCLE

How a phishing attack works

01 Attacker Creates the attack
→
02 Fake Message Email / SMS / DM
→
03 Victim Interaction Clicks or responds
→
04 Fake Page Information requested
→
05 Exposure Information stolen
WARNING SIGNS

Remember these red flags

⚠️ Unexpected urgency
⚠️ Suspicious sender
⚠️ Strange links
⚠️ Requests for OTP/password
⚠️ Unrealistic rewards
⚠️ Unusual payment requests

MODULE 07

Phishing Case Studies

Understand how phishing appears in real-world situations.

📚
CASE 01

Password Expiry Scam

A user receives a message saying their company account password is about to expire. The message contains a link to a login page.

Lesson Do not use the message link. Open the known official service yourself.
CASE 02

Business Payment Request

An employee receives an urgent request appearing to come from a manager asking for a payment to a new bank account.

Lesson Verify unusual payment requests using a separate trusted communication channel.
CASE 03

Delivery / Tax Message

A message claims that a package is waiting and a small tax or delivery fee must be paid immediately through a link.

Lesson Verify the delivery directly through the official website or application.
KEY TAKEAWAY

Verify through a trusted route

1

Do not trust the contact information inside the suspicious message.

2

Find the official website or known contact details independently.

3

For sensitive requests, confirm through a separate trusted channel.

MODULE 08

Security Tips

Build habits that reduce your risk of becoming a phishing victim.

🔐
01
🔑

Use Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for important accounts. A password manager can help generate and store them.

02
🛡

Enable MFA / Passkeys

Multi-factor authentication provides another layer of protection if a password is exposed.

03
🔗

Verify Links

Check the destination and prefer opening services directly instead of trusting unexpected links.

04
⏸

Pause Under Pressure

Urgency is commonly used to stop people from thinking before acting.

05
🔢

Never Share OTPs

Legitimate support staff should not ask you to disclose your OTP or authentication code.

06
🚨

Report Suspicious Messages

Report suspicious emails or messages using your organisation's security process or the service's reporting feature.

MODULE 09

I Got Phished — What Now?

Quick response actions can reduce the damage after an incident.

🚨
🖱

I clicked, but entered nothing

  1. Close the suspicious page.
  2. Do not download or run anything.
  3. Report the message if appropriate.
  4. Run security checks if a download occurred.
🔑

I entered my password

  1. Change the password using the legitimate service.
  2. Change the same password anywhere it was reused.
  3. Enable MFA or passkeys.
  4. Review active sessions and account activity.
💳

I entered financial information

  1. Contact your bank/provider through an official channel.
  2. Monitor transactions and account activity.
  3. Follow the provider's fraud-response instructions.
  4. Report the incident if required.
⚠️ Important: Be careful of people who contact you claiming they can “recover” your account for a fee. Attackers may use the original incident to run a second scam.
